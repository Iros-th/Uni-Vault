# Nebula Hive Architecture

## Overview
Nebula is a decentralized quantitative trading hive mind and autonomous strategy discovery engine created by Ross Hayes (@ross_rfx). ## Core Pillars of the Nebula Architecture

### 1. The Law of Large Numbers & Polaris Hive
- **Collective Prior**: Rather than individual retail traders overfitting isolated backtests, all runs feed into the central Polaris decision engine.
- **Pooled Bias Neutrality**: Evaluates whether strategy families share a genuine edge (`0 of 14 families lean one way - neutral prior`) across hundreds of thousands of recorded outcomes.
- **Network Validation**: Live and simulated forward checks are never combined, but pooled across the entire network to measure empirical stability.

### 2. Autonomous Mode ("Set a Goal, Walk Away")
Users configure an objective (e.g., *"find me three systems better than what I have on gold, don't take more than 12h"*), and Nebula autonomously executes tests across 6 analytical dimensions:
1. **Pair and Timeframe Heat Map**: Exhaustive multi-cell search ranking comparative performance across selected assets and timeframes (M15 to Daily).
2. **Walk-Forward Study**: Stability testing at 50%, 60%, 70%, and 80% in-sample shares to verify pass count persistence.
3. **Overfit Audit**: Mathematically rigorous overfit verification computing Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO) and Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) spread without speculative search bias.
4. **Style Shoot-Out**: Multi-style competition (momentum, breakout, mean-reversion, neutral arbitrage) on identical asset cells.
5. **Regime Study**: Shared-window regime matrix identifying which macro market conditions produce maximum alpha.
6. **Network Versus Mine**: Local backtest / forward test benchmarking directly against the hive network's accumulated prior.
