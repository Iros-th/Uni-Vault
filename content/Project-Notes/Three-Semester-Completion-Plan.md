# DTU Three-Semester Completion Plan (BSc Matematik & Teknologi)

> [!abstract] Overview & Objective
> This plan organizes your entire **BSc in Matematik og Teknologi / Anvendt Matematik foundation (80–85 ECTS)** into an intensive, airtight **3-semester completion timeline** (1.5 academic years).
>
> By utilizing DTU's standard **13-week semester + 3-week intensive term (January & June)** model, you take a manageable 25–30 ECTS per semester without timetable clashes or unsatisfied prerequisites.

---

## High-Level Pacing Overview

| Semester | Season / Period | 13-Week Courses (ECTS) | 3-Week Intensive (ECTS) | Total ECTS |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Semester 1** | **Autumn 1** (Sep–Jan) | Mat1a (10) + Diskret (5) + Programmering (5) + Intro MatTech Pt. 1 (5) | Intro MatTech Pt. 2 / Project (5) | **30 ECTS** |
| **Semester 2** | **Spring 1** (Feb–Jun) | Mat1b (10) + Statistik (5) + Physics Pt. 1 (5) + Algoritmer & Datastrukturer (5) | Kemi / Grundlæggende Kemi Lab (5) | **30 ECTS** |
| **Semester 3** | **Autumn 2** (Sep–Jan) | Physics Pt. 2 (5) + Numeriske Algoritmer (5) + Diskret 2: Algebra (5) + Valgfag (5) | Matematisk Modellering (5) | **25–30 ECTS** |
| **TOTALS** | **1.5 Years** | **70 ECTS** | **15 ECTS** | **85–90 ECTS** |

## Detailed Semester Breakdown

### Semester 1 (Autumn 1: September – January) — Total: 30 ECTS
*Theme: The Proof, Logic, Linear Algebra & Core Programming Cluster.*

1. **`01003` Mathematics 1a (Polytechnical Foundation)** — **10 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `E5 + E3B` (Wednesdays 8–17 + Friday afternoons 13–17)
   - **Content:** Vectors, systems of linear equations, matrices, determinants, vector spaces, linear mappings, eigenvalues, complex numbers.
   - **Role:** Core prerequisite for Mat1b, Physics, and Numerical Algorithms.

2. **`01017` Discrete Mathematics** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `E2B` (Thursday mornings 8–12)
   - **Content:** Propositional logic, sets, relations, functions, induction, combinatorics, graph theory.
   - **Synergy:** Heavy overlap with 02525 proof techniques and Mat1a induction.

3. **`02002` Computer Programming (Polytechnical Foundation)** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `E1B` (Thursday afternoons 13–17)
   - **Content:** Python programming from fundamentals, data structures, algorithm design.
   - **Synergy:** Prepares toolset used in Numerical Algorithms and Algorithms & Data Structures.

4. **`02525` Introduction to Mathematics and Technology (Part 1)** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `E2A` (Monday afternoons 13–17)
   - **Content:** Rigorous mathematical thinking, proofs (Brander text), analysis, technology applications.

5. **`02525` January Intensive Period (Part 2)** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `January 3-Week Term` (Full-time intensive)
   - **Content:** Hands-on integrative project synthesizing autumn theory.

> [!check] Schedule Conflict Check (Semester 1)
> - **Monday:** 13–17 (`02525` E2A)
> - **Wednesday:** 8–17 (`01003` E5)
> - **Thursday:** 8–12 (`01017` E2B) | 13–17 (`02002` E1B)
> - **Friday:** 13–17 (`01003` E3B)
> - **Result:** **Zero collisions.** Every slot is distinct.

### Semester 2 (Spring 1: February – June) — Total: 30 ECTS
*Theme: The Calculus Continuum, Statistical Modeling & Algorithmic Rigour.*

1. **`01004` Mathematics 1b (Polytechnical Foundation)** — **10 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `F5 + F3B` (Wednesdays 8–17 + Friday afternoons 13–17)
   - **Prerequisites:** Passed `01003` (Mat1a).
   - **Content:** Multivariate calculus, partial derivatives, multiple integrals, vector fields, Taylor polynomials, infinite series, differential equations (ODEs).

2. **`02402` Statistics (Polytechnical Foundation)** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `F4A` (Tuesday afternoons 13–17)
   - **Prerequisites:** Uses integration and combinatorics from Semester 1.
   - **Content:** Probability distributions, hypothesis testing, linear regression, confidence intervals, sample theory.

3. **`10060` Physics (Part 1)** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `F2A / F1A` (Spans Spring + Autumn, 13+13 wks)
   - **Content:** Mechanics, vectors, Newtonian dynamics, oscillations, work and energy.
   - **Synergy:** Directly applies the ODEs and calculus taught simultaneously in Mat1b.

4. **`02105` Algorithms & Data Structures 1 (Elective Slot B)** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `F2B` (Thursday mornings 8–12)
   - **Prerequisites:** Passed Python (`02002`) and Discrete Math (`01017`).
   - **Content:** Sorting, search trees, graphs, asymptotic runtime complexity, dynamic programming.

5. **General Chemistry (Polytechnical Foundation)** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `June 3-Week Term`
   - **Content:** Chemical stoichiometry, equilibrium, thermodynamics, laboratory safety and reporting.
   - **Advantage:** Taking chemistry as a 3-week June block avoids crowding the 13-week schedule and gets hands-on lab requirements completed in one focused burst.

> [!check] Schedule Conflict Check (Semester 2)
> - **Tuesday:** 13–17 (`02402` F4A)
> - **Wednesday:** 8–17 (`01004` F5)
> - **Thursday:** 8–12 (`02105` F2B)
> - **Friday:** 13–17 (`01004` F3B)
> - **Physics:** Handled in open morning windows (F1A/F2A).
> - **Result:** **Zero collisions.**

### Semester 3 (Autumn 2: September – January) — Total: 25–30 ECTS
*Theme: Advanced Computational Mathematics, Algebraic Structures & Capstone Modeling.*

1. **`10060` Physics (Part 2)** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `Autumn 13-Week`
   - **Content:** Electromagnetism, circuits, wave mechanics, laboratory reports. Completes the 10 ECTS physics sequence.

2. **`02601` Introduction to Numerical Algorithms** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `E4B` (Friday mornings 8–12, English edition)
   - **Prerequisites:** Passed Mat1b (`01004`) and Python (`02002`).
   - **Content:** Root finding, numerical ODE integration, spline interpolation, conditioning and floating point stability.

3. **`01018` Discrete Mathematics 2: Algebra (Elective Slot A)** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `E3B` (Friday afternoons 13–17)
   - **Prerequisites:** Passed `01017` and `01003`.
   - **Content:** Groups, rings, fields, polynomial rings, modular arithmetic, RSA cryptography.
   - **Synergy:** Minimum friction elective because 70% of concepts build straight on 01017 and Mat1a.

4. **Technical Elective / Bachelor Specialization Course** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** Open slot (e.g. `E1A`, `E2B`, or `E3A`).
   - **Recommendations:** Optimization, Machine Learning fundamentals, or Data Analysis.

5. **`02526` Mathematical Modeling (Integrative Capstone)** — **5 ECTS**
   - **Schedule Group:** `January 3-Week Term`
   - **Prerequisites:** Mat1b, Numerical Algorithms (`02601`), Statistics (`02402`), Programming (`02002`), Physics (`10060`).
   - **Content:** Formulating real-world technological challenges into mathematical equations, solving them numerically, and communicating results.
   - **Role:** Pulls the entire 1.5-year mathematical and scientific toolbox together as the official capstone!

> [!check] Schedule Conflict Check (Semester 3)
> - **Friday 8–12:** `02601` (E4B)
> - **Friday 13–17:** `01018` (E3B)
> - **Physics:** Spring/Autumn distributed.
> - **January:** `02526` dedicated 3-week block.
> - **Result:** **Zero collisions.**

## Critical Execution Rules & Dependencies

1. **The Core Pipeline Cannot Be Reordered:**
   $$\text{Mat1a (01003)} \to \text{Mat1b (01004)} \to \text{Numerical (02601)} \to \text{Modelling (02526)}$$
2. **Take Advantage of the 3-Week Blocks:**
   DTU's calendar gives you January and June. Using them for `02525 (Jan 1)`, `Chemistry (June 1)`, and `02526 (Jan 2)` is the key mechanism that makes completing 85 ECTS in 3 semesters entirely realistic and stress-free.
3. **Synergy Multipliers:**
   - Grouping `02525 + 01017 + 01003` in Semester 1 means you learn proof structure and induction once, gaining instant compounding mastery.
   - Taking `02601` immediately following `Mat1b` ensures differential equations and vector calculus are completely fresh.
