# Terms: Introduction to applied mathematics

The core terms for this course. Full definitions live in the [[Glossary]] and the maths reference pages.

## Proof basics

### Definition, theorem, proof
The building blocks of a mathematical argument. See [[Reference/Matematik/Matematiske begreber|Maths terms]].

### Axiom
A statement accepted without proof.

## Proof methods

### Direct proof, contrapositive, contradiction
The standard ways to prove an implication. See [[Glossary]].

### Induction
Proving a statement for all integers from a base case and an inductive step.

## Functions and sets

### Injective, surjective, bijective
Properties of maps between sets. See [[Glossary]].

### Number sets and symbols
$\mathbb{N}, \mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Q}, \mathbb{R}, \mathbb{C}$ and logical notation. See [[Reference/Matematik/Talmaengder|Number sets]] and [[Reference/Matematik/Matematiske symboler|Maths symbols]].
