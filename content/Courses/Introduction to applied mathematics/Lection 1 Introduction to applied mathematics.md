What is a proof 
Examples of types of argument:
	1: Direct Proof
	2: Counter examples
	3: Proof by contradiction (indirect)



Proof Definition
	1: Axioms - an axiom is a foundational statement or rule that is accepted as true without requiring a proof
		Example: addition of real numbers is commutative, f.x. $a + b = b + a$
		When showing that we use axioms in our theory please show why the theory needs said axiom 
		also when using axioms in you theory you typically use axioms + other theory that is proven 
	2: "Valid" logical arguments to prove other facts that are not obvious
		Example: let $m, n \in \mathbb{Z}$ be integers
		claim: $\int_{0}^{2\pi} \cos(nx)\cos(mx)\,dx = 0$ if $m \ne n$, and $\pi$ if $m = n$
	3: Proof by Counter Example
		example: the claim that $\int_{0}^{2\pi} \cos(nx)\cos(mx)\,dx = 0$ for all integers $m, n$
	4: prove or disprove 
		Example: "Every degree 2 polynomial has a real root" is not true, as $x^2 + 1 = 0$ makes $x = i$
	5: Proof by Contradiction
	6: proof of theorem suppose
