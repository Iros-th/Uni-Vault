# Terms: Differential Equations and Infinite Series

Core terms for the ODE and series course. Full explanations and formulas live in the reference pages and the formula collection.

## Differential equations

### Differential equation
An equation relating a function to its derivatives. See [[Reference/Formelsamling/Differentialligninger|Differentialligninger]].

### Order
The order of the highest derivative that appears in the equation.

### Linear ODE
An equation where the unknown function and its derivatives appear only to the first power and are not multiplied together.

### General and particular solution
The general solution carries the free constants; a particular solution fixes them from initial conditions.

### Separable equation
An equation you can rewrite so each variable sits on its own side, then integrate both.

## Infinite series

### Infinite series
The sum of infinitely many terms, defined as the limit of its partial sums.

### Convergence
A series converges when its partial sums approach a finite limit.

### Power series
A series in powers of $(x - a)$, a function built from infinitely many polynomial terms.

### Radius of convergence
The distance $R$ around the centre within which a power series converges.

### Taylor series
A power series that reconstructs a smooth function from its derivatives at one point.
