# Self-test: Differential Equations and Infinite Series

Answer each one yourself first, then click "Show answer". No score is kept, this is just for you.

**1.** What is the order of the equation $y'' + 3y' + 2y = 0$?

> [!success]- Show answer
> Second order, because the highest derivative that appears is $y''$.

**2.** Solve $y' = ky$.

> [!success]- Show answer
> $y = Ce^{kx}$. This is exponential growth or decay, with $C$ fixed by an initial condition.

**3.** What makes an equation separable, and what do you do with it?

> [!success]- Show answer
> You can rewrite it so each variable sits on its own side, for example $\frac{dy}{g(y)} = f(x)\,dx$, then integrate both sides.

**4.** Does the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n}$ converge?

> [!success]- Show answer
> No. The harmonic series diverges, even though its terms go to zero. Terms going to zero is necessary but not sufficient for convergence.

**5.** What is the radius of convergence, and where is a power series guaranteed to converge?

> [!success]- Show answer
> The radius $R$ is the distance around the centre $a$ within which the series converges. It always converges for $|x - a| < R$ and diverges for $|x - a| > R$.

**6.** Write the Taylor series of $e^x$ about $0$.

> [!success]- Show answer
> $e^x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!} = 1 + x + \frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{x^3}{6} + \cdots$.
